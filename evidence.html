Preregistration

Preregistered predictions, and what missed

Each instrument's predictions were committed to version control before its closed-loop data existed. Three revisions to the disposal rules happened later than that, and the paper records each one with its timing: the interval form of one rule was entered after its paired analysis ran, and a reproduction criterion was redefined mid-stream after two identical runs differed at float precision. Three preregistered predictions missed and a fourth is unscored, because the ambient arms it compares against were never re-run under the current code. All four are in the paper, and each narrowed what it claims.
